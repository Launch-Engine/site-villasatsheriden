Discover the Luxury of The Villas at Sheriden.

Explore your interests and embrace your purpose at The Villas at Sheriden. Every detail in our modern and pet-friendly community in Carey, Ohio, was thoughtfully designed with your convenience and unique needs in mind. From the beautiful finishes to the stainless steal appliances and the abundance of natural light, you’ll have everything you need to enjoy the stylish and convenient one-story living. 

Start your day on your gorgeous patio taking in the warm morning sun. Then head to the convenient community room; a great place to meet neighbors for a quick card game and other social activities, or just relax and read a good book. Enjoy a well-equipped fitness center just steps from your front door, or get your hands dirty nurturing plants in the community garden.

Join us at The Villas at Sheriden. Enjoy community, connection and purpose. Check out our photo gallery to sneak a peek at our pet-friendly apartments, then schedule a visit to tour them in person!
