Get acquainted with the rewarding living experience you’ll enjoy at The Villas at Sheriden by embarking on a visual tour. Take a look at our luxurious, yet comfortable, apartments, thoughtful features and all of the wonderful amenities that are included and imagine your life here.

Beautiful inside and out, our apartment community has everything you need to be fit, stay active, be social and have fun. When you want to stretch or push your physical limits, the fully equipped fitness center is there for you. For fun-filled afternoons in the company of your friends, family or neighbors, the community room is your go-to place. The apartments are comfortable and exemplify modern touches of sophistication. With modern cabinetry and cozy patios, our homes put the emphasis on your wellbeing. Other attractive features include luxury vinyl flooring, large closets and stainless-steel appliances. They’re also pet friendly.
