Welcome to The Villas at Sheriden


LOVE WHERE YOU LIVE!

 

At The Villas at Sheriden, your purpose is our purpose, and we invite you to engage curiously and wholeheartedly in creating the life you want. With beautiful interiors and layouts and a large list of attractive amenities, our new 55+ community caters to those who want the convenience of apartment living with additional amenities such as a community garden, clubhouse and fitness center. You'll love the life you live at The Villas at Sheriden!

 

Choose between spacious one or two bedroom floor plans with patios and lots of natural light. We’ve artfully used every inch of space to create an open and airy floor plan sprinkled with features that are both tasteful and practical.

 

GET CONNECTED.

We are not your average apartment complex. We understand that neighbors make a community a home. That’s why we make it easy to enjoy a cup of coffee in the community room with friends or just relax and read a good book. Like staying active? Enjoy a quick workout in the fitness center, just steps from your front door. Looking for a place to have a family gathering or get together with friends? Reserve the community room for all of life's special occasions.  Avid and novice gardeners alike will love working together in the community garden while sharing the wealth with neighbors.  There is something for everyone at The Villas at Sheriden.
