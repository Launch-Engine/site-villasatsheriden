Map & Directions

When you live at The Villas at Sheriden, you’ll have everything you need within reach. Located at 925 Sheriden Drive, our community is just moments from beautiful nature sites, local dining and places of worship. Enjoy beautiful views with the convenience of easy access to US 23 for larger shopping and entertainment areas.  Experience all of the joys of a small midwestern community, with the convenience of larger surrounding neighbors. Travel just a few miles in any direction to explore the possibilities in nearby Findlay, Upper Sandusky or Tiffin.

Excitement awaits at home, as well. From cozy afternoons spent on your patio to fun evenings in the community room, you will never run out of things to do. Not to mention we also have a fitness center and community garden to explore.

We’re sure you’ll love living at The Villas at Sheriden, so schedule a tour today!
